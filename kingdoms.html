KINGDOMS

Menzoberranzan

Beneath hollow mountains and caverns that breathe darkness lies Menzoberranzan: the capital of the fallen lineage, cradle of the Void Elves. It is not merely a city; it is a wound in the world. Its twisted towers gleam with a pale light, shaped by magic that does not create but consumes. Shadows move even when no one walks; the architecture responds to thought rather than to hand. Here, survival is law. Its inhabitants descend from the ancient primordial exiles, those whom the light forgot and who heeded the whisper of the Void. Their white eyes watch every corner, capable of seeing what the darkness attempts to conceal.

Alfheim

Alfheim is the heart of the living world: forests that breathe, rivers that sing, and cities built from branches that never die. Here dwell the Wood Elves, direct descendants of those who offered their divine blood to Mother Nature in exchange for true eternity.

Valinor

Valinor is the purest echo of what the Primordial Elves were before their fall: a land where light does not burn but caresses; where time does not destroy but beautifies. It is home to the descendants closest to the primordial lineage, those whose blood still resonates with divine cadence. Here, memory does not burden; it flows. Beauty is not displayed; it simply exists. Magic is not invoked; it is remembered, as though the world were obeying an ancient instinct
